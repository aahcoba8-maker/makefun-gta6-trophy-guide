## What this guide does

Leonida Playbook brings all 55 PlayStation trophies into one offline tracker, with original, source-linked guidance, a seven-stage route to the platinum, a clear warning for the one missable and six story-sensitive chains, and browser-only progress you can export or import.

An offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI: every trophy with its official name, description and grade, a guide to each written in our own words and citing its sources, a seven-stage roadmap to the platinum, the one missable trophy flagged up front, and a checklist that saves your progress in the browser and exports/imports it as a file. The app is plain static files under `app/` — open `app/index.html` in a desktop browser, no network needed.


## What changed in v2

Each trophy now has its own rewritten guide instead of sharing a template. The duplicate platinum badge is gone, and the checklist type is larger for easier reading.
